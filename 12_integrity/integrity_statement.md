# Scientific Integrity Statement

## Project Title

Artificial Intelligence Model for Credit Risk Classification of Peruvian Municipal Savings Banks using Random Forest

---

## Research Integrity

This project was developed following the principles of scientific integrity, transparency and reproducible research.

Every stage of the Machine Learning workflow, including data preparation, preprocessing, model training, evaluation and interpretation, is documented within this repository.

---

## Data Transparency

The dataset consists of publicly available financial indicators published by the Superintendencia de Banca, Seguros y AFP (SBS) of Peru.

No confidential, private or personally identifiable information was collected or processed.

---

## Reproducibility

The complete workflow is reproducible.

The repository includes:

- Dataset
- Python notebook
- Trained Random Forest model
- Label Encoder
- Scaler
- Generated figures
- Evaluation results
- Documentation

The required Python libraries are listed in requirements.txt.

---

## Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence tools were used exclusively to assist with programming, documentation and technical writing.

All methodological decisions, implementation details, model selection, interpretation of results and conclusions were reviewed and validated by the author.

---

## Ethical Compliance

The study analyzes institutional financial indicators only.

No protected demographic variables such as gender, ethnicity, religion or age are included.

Consequently, the study does not involve discrimination against individuals or sensitive social groups.

---

## Scientific Transparency

The repository provides complete documentation of:

- preprocessing
- feature engineering
- model configuration
- evaluation metrics
- cross validation
- feature importance

allowing independent verification of the reported results.

---

## Limitations

The conclusions are limited to the analyzed dataset.

Future research should include additional financial institutions, larger historical datasets and external validation.

---

## Declaration

The author declares that this work was conducted honestly, transparently and in accordance with the principles of scientific integrity and responsible Artificial Intelligence research.
